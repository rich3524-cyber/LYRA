Demo Reference Guide

Key talking points, demo flow, and quick facts for the LYRA platform walkthrough.

The pitch

LYRA is the only social media management platform that responds to comments and reviews autonomously, 24/7 — with configurable AI autonomy and agency-controlled guardrails ensuring every response reflects the brand accurately. It also surfaces scheduled email campaigns from Klaviyo and Mailchimp directly on the social calendar, so agencies see every outbound channel in a single view. Everything else — scheduling, captions, analytics — competitors do. The comment response engine and unified marketing calendar are why LYRA exists.

Recommended demo flow

Walkthrough sequence

  • 01

    Brand Intelligence — "The Foundation"

    Show the Voice Summary, Tone Attributes, Content Themes, and Audience Profile. Built by crawling the website and analysing the social feed. Takes 2 minutes. Refreshes weekly automatically. Key point: every AI feature downstream — captions, comment responses, content scoring, repurposing, client reports — is grounded in this profile. Nothing is generic, everything is calibrated to the specific brand.

  • 02

    Compose — "Write Once, Post Everywhere"

    Select multiple platforms at the top, write once. Show AI caption generation — 3 variations calibrated to the brand voice profile. Show the Content Score panel updating live as you type — 6 dimensions, score of 7+ means ready to schedule, below 7 shows specific fix suggestions. Then show Repurpose Content: paste a blog URL, LYRA generates a LinkedIn article, Instagram caption, Twitter thread, and Facebook post simultaneously — each adapted for that platform's format, character limits, and tone.

  • 03

    Calendar — "The Full Marketing Picture"

    Monthly grid showing every scheduled and published social post — colour-coded by status, filterable by platform. New: Klaviyo and Mailchimp email campaigns appear directly on the calendar as violet entries alongside social posts — so you can see an email newsletter landing the same week as a social push without switching tools. Drag any social post to reschedule (time preserved). Click Generate schedule: set 3 or 6 weeks, pick platforms, set posts per week — LYRA writes the entire plan with AI-generated captions, streaming in real time. Every post goes through a review screen before it touches the calendar.

  • 04

    Inbox — "The Wow Moment" — spend the most time here

    Every comment, reply, and review from every connected platform, pulled in every 5 minutes. Each one sentiment-scored automatically. AI drafts a response for every single one using the brand voice profile and guardrails. Show all three autonomy modes: Off (inbox only, human writes everything) → Draft + Approve (AI drafts, human approves before posting) → Full Autonomy (AI generates and posts directly — responses live within ~2 minutes, no human step). Show an Always Escalate rule catching a sensitive keyword and routing to human review. Show an Approved Answer overriding AI generation for a pricing question with a pre-written factual response.

  • 05

    Crisis Aware — "The Safety Net That Knows the Business"

    Two triggers: a sentiment spike across incoming comments, or a match against the workspace's escalation keyword list. The differentiator: that keyword list isn't a generic template — Brand Intelligence generates it automatically every time the brand profile is built, tailored to this specific business (legal threats, safety incidents, discrimination claims, media inquiries, plus business-specific terms like "food poisoning" for a restaurant or "data breach" for a software company). Show it on the Brand Intelligence page: suggested keywords appear as chips to approve or dismiss, plus a manual add field. When either trigger fires, all scheduled posts pause automatically — nothing publishes during an active crisis — and every workspace owner/admin gets a real email alert, immediately, with the triggering comment and a link straight into the Inbox. No one has to be watching the dashboard to find out. Triggering comments surface in the inbox, fully escalated comments can be replied to directly from there, not just viewed. Every event is audit-logged with trigger type, timestamp, and comment IDs. Disable Crisis Mode once resolved; posts resume. The scenario to tell: client posts something controversial on a Friday night — LYRA pauses their entire scheduled queue automatically and emails you immediately, while you sleep. Nothing tone-deaf goes out, and you find out the moment it happens, not whenever you next open the dashboard.

  • 06

    Analytics + Reports — "Proving the Value"

    Cross-platform dashboard: reach, impressions, engagements, engagement rate. Competitor Intelligence: track up to 5 competitors per workspace — posts per week, engagement benchmarks, content topics, updated weekly automatically. Client Performance Reports: one click → branded PDF → summary metrics, platform breakdown, best post, AI-written performance narrative, competitor benchmarks. Ready in seconds. 7-day or 30-day period. Agency plan.

  • 07

    LYRA Trend (not yet available — do not demo)

    Planned add-on: daily AI-scored trend discovery, matched to the workspace's brand, one click to carry into the composer. Not built yet — no Trend Hub, no discovery sync, no composer integration. Checkout is disabled. Do not walk a prospect through this until it actually ships.

AI autonomy

The three modes

Mode What happens Plan
Off Inbox only. All responses are written manually. AI does not draft anything. All plans
Draft + Approve AI drafts a response for every comment. Agency or client reviews and approves before it posts. Nothing goes live without a human seeing it. Pro + Agency
Full Autonomy AI generates and posts responses directly — no human review. Responses live within ~2 minutes. Guardrails and escalation rules are the control layer. Agency only

Clients cannot set their own autonomy ceiling. The agency controls the maximum. A client workspace can only reach Draft + Approve — Full Autonomy requires the agency to explicitly enable it.

Guardrails (Agency plan)

Type Effect
Never DiscussAI will never engage with this topic — e.g. competitor names, pricing, pending litigation.
Never Use WordSpecific words or phrases the AI must never use — e.g. "cheap", brand-specific language standards.
Always EscalateAny comment matching this trigger routes to human review regardless of autonomy setting — e.g. "refund", "lawyer", "allergic reaction". This is also the same keyword list Crisis Aware watches — Brand Intelligence now suggests it automatically, tailored to the business.
Approved AnswersPre-written responses used verbatim when a specific question is asked — opening hours, pricing, FAQs. Ensures factual accuracy.

Common objections

Quick answers

"We already use Hootsuite / Buffer / Sprout Social."

None of them respond to comments — and none show your email campaigns alongside your social posts in a single calendar. LYRA handles what they all ignore, and does the scheduling too.

"We already use Klaviyo / Mailchimp for email."

Good — LYRA connects to both. Your email campaigns appear on the LYRA calendar alongside your social posts. You keep using Klaviyo or Mailchimp to build and send campaigns; LYRA just gives you the unified view so you can see everything at once. No migration, no disruption.

"What if the AI says something wrong?"

Draft + Approve means nothing goes live without a human seeing it. Guardrails are a second layer — specific topics, words, and triggers are locked down at the agency level. Always Escalate rules catch anything sensitive before the AI touches it.

"Is it safe for clients to have Full Autonomy?"

Clients cannot set their own autonomy level. The agency controls the ceiling. A client workspace can only reach Draft + Approve — Full Autonomy requires the agency to explicitly enable it per workspace.

"What happens to scheduled posts during a crisis?"

They pause automatically the moment Crisis Mode activates. Nothing publishes. You resolve the situation, disable Crisis Mode, and posts resume from where they were.

"Can you customise responses per platform?"

Yes — the composer defaults to a shared caption but any platform tab can be unlocked and edited independently. The repurposing tool generates platform-native variants from the start, each adapted for that channel's format and audience.

"Do you just surface random trends — how is it relevant to our brand?"

Every trend the client sees has already been filtered through their brand intelligence profile. LYRA doesn't show what's trending globally — it shows what's trending for this specific brand. Each trend has a relevance score and an AI-written explanation of why it fits. Low-scoring trends are included for visibility but the high-confidence matches are ranked first. It's the difference between a fire hose and a briefing.
